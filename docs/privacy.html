Privacy Policy

Park That Thought ("the app") is a free, open source Windows app made by G M Iqbal Mahmud. Last updated: 5 October 2026.

The short version

Everything the app does happens on your PC. There is no server and no account. The developer never receives your notes, your calendar or any other data.

Your notes

Your notes, lists and settings are saved only in the app's data folder on your PC. They are not uploaded anywhere.

Google Calendar (optional)

If you choose "Connect with Google", the app asks Google for permission to:

  • See your list of calendars (names and colors), so you can pick which ones to show.
  • See and add events in your calendars, so the app can show your upcoming meetings (title, time, location, description and meeting link) and add an event you type into the app.
  • See your email address, so the app can show which Google account is connected.
  • Keep a private app folder in your Google Drive, used only if you turn on sync with the Park That Thought phone app, to copy your own notes between your devices. The app cannot see any other file in your Drive.

The app talks to Google directly from your PC. Events are kept in memory while the app runs and are not saved to disk. The sign-in token is saved on your PC, encrypted with Windows so that only your Windows account can read it.

The app does not sell, share or transfer your Google data to anyone, does not use it for ads, and does not use it to train AI models. Park That Thought's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Stopping access

In the app, open Settings, Calendar and click Disconnect. This deletes the saved token from your PC. You can also remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Updates

The app checks GitHub for a newer version. That request includes no personal data.
